## Critical Evaluation

Fase 1 was largely AI-assisted and reviewed against the repository's Clean Architecture guard. The first validation exposed missing local dependencies and one incorrect type import; both were corrected before the final checks. The team must still decide the concrete mode descriptors and factories in the later integration phase. During the 2026-11-24 SOLID refactor, the new acyclic-dependency architecture gate caught a real circular barrel import (`value-objects/index.ts` ↔ `game-mode-descriptor.ts`), and the initial `max-lines` error threshold (250) was relaxed to `warn` (350) after human review.
